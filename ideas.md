# Waitlist Page Design Direction

## Three Initial Approaches

### 1. Powder Room Editorial

**Very Brief Intro:** A white-field fashion launch page that treats the supplied blush pink as a collectible material: soft, tactile, and sparingly placed. The mood is quiet, modern, and slightly surreal.

**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. Newsprint Atelier

**Very Brief Intro:** A sharp black-and-white fashion flyer layout interrupted by one airy pink swatch. The feel would be youthful, archival, and typographically assertive.

**Probability:** 0.03

### 3. Glossed Pop Gallery

**Very Brief Intro:** A playful gallery-like page with oversized candy-pink forms and tiny editorial labels. It would lean expressive and collectible while retaining abundant white space.

**Probability:** 0.09

---

## Chosen Approach: Powder Room Editorial

### Design Movement

Contemporary fashion editorial meets **New Romantic minimalism**: airy white composition, art-directed texture, and a single soft shade used as a material rather than a decorative color.

### Core Principles

1. **White space carries the luxury.** Empty space is intentional and makes every word and pink object feel curated.
2. **Asymmetry creates anticipation.** The form, headline, notes, and artwork occupy different visual weights rather than sitting in a conventional centered card.
3. **The pink is tactile, not loud.** It appears as fabric, a blurred orb, markers, and controlled accent fills—not wide decorative gradients.
4. **Type is the primary product.** The hierarchy is direct and editorial, using SF Pro Display styling to bring confident clarity.

### Color Philosophy

The base is pure white, representing a blank lookbook page. The signature blush pink, **#F39BE2**, echoes the supplied reference and carries softness, optimism, and material presence. Ink is near-black (#151313) instead of absolute black to retain a printed-page warmth. Pale rose paper (#FFF5FD) is used only behind small moments of interaction.

### Layout Paradigm

The page follows an **off-center editorial spread**: a fine horizontal masthead at the top, a large left-aligned manifesto anchoring the lower-left, a freestanding sign-up unit just off the main headline, and an oversized pink sculpture interrupting the right edge. This creates the feeling of a magazine cover caught in a moment rather than a centralised marketing template.

### Signature Elements

1. A deliberately cropped, airbrushed blush orb on the page edge.
2. A small folded-petal symbol used as the brand marker, form success mark, and favicon.
3. Micro editorial captions connected by thin ink rules and pink registration dots.

### Interaction Philosophy

The interaction model is focused: visitors select email or phone, enter one contact detail, and join. The selected contact option behaves like a quiet material tab; the join button provides a concise status shift rather than excessive celebration.

### Animation

On entry, the logo, editorial eyebrow, and headline fade upward in staggered 60ms intervals, while the orb eases in from the right over 700ms. The form panel rises a few pixels only. Hovered controls use a 160ms transform and color transition; pressing a button slightly compresses it. Motion is disabled for reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System

Use the **SF Pro Display / -apple-system** family first, with a precise system fallback. The heading is in a 700–800 weight with very tight tracking and a fluid scale; section labels are uppercase at small sizes with expanded tracking; supporting copy stays at a restrained 14–16px with generous line height. No generic geometric sans is introduced.

### Brand Essence

**A fashion label’s private first look, for people who want to arrive before the release.**

Personality: **considered, tactile, self-assured**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are short, declarative, and a little elusive. CTAs are confident but never over-explain. Microcopy feels like a note from the studio rather than a marketing automation.

Example lines:

> The first look is almost yours.

> Leave your number. We’ll keep it close.

### Wordmark & Logo

The working wordmark is rendered as a custom typographic masthead—**THE FIRST EDIT**—with tracked, sharply spaced letters. The logo mark is an abstract folded-petal rosette, a visual reference to gathered fabric and the supplied circular blush form. It never relies on a default-font text lockup.

### Signature Brand Color

**Powder Signal — #F39BE2.**
